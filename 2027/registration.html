What Does Registration Include?

Full-Paper Registration — On-site

  • Presentation of the accepted paper during the conference.
  • Access to all conference sessions.
  • Lunches and coffee breaks during the conference.
  • Conference kit, including a notebook, pen, and other registration materials.
  • Certificate of presentation.
  • Processing of the paper for submission to the conference proceedings and consideration for inclusion in IEEE Xplore, subject to applicable IEEE requirements.

Abstract Registration — On-site

  • Oral or poster presentation of the accepted abstract, according to the conference program.
  • Access to conference sessions.
  • Lunches and coffee breaks during the conference.
  • Conference kit, including a notebook, pen, and other registration materials.
  • Certificate of presentation.
  • Abstract inclusion in the conference program. Abstract-only submissions are not considered full papers for publication in the proceedings.

Online Registration — Full Paper or Abstract

  • Remote presentation of the accepted full paper or abstract, according to the approved presentation format.
  • Online access to the conference sessions and available virtual conference services.
  • Certificate of presentation, subject to the conference's attendance and presentation requirements.
  • For full papers, eligibility for consideration for inclusion in IEEE Xplore remains subject to the same applicable conference and IEEE requirements.
